# Page backlog

Ideas for later pages. Each one should use the same generator, the same disclaimer, and official sources only. Do not add forum or social-media wait times.

1. Title and register a vehicle after moving to Florida. The FLHSMV new-resident page states a 10-day registration rule, Florida insurance, lienholder steps, and who may inspect the vehicle identification number. The three tax collector sites add local office rules. Keep this separate from the license-transfer page.

2. Commercial driver license transfer. Cover the extra Florida residency documents, the ban on the Certification of Address for a commercial learner's permit or commercial driver license, and which Broward, Miami-Dade, and Palm Beach offices actually give commercial tests. FLHSMV location pages and the county tax collector pages disagree on details, so quote both.

3. Replace a lost, stolen, or damaged Florida license or ID card. Section 322.21 and the FLHSMV fee schedule list a replacement fee. Palm Beach lists no fee for a stolen card when its documents are provided. State that difference and the in-person versus MyDMV Portal path.

4. Change the address on a Florida license within 30 days. Section 322.19 and the county pages cover the deadline, which changes can be done on MyDMV Portal, and which still need an office visit and two proofs of the new address.

5. Learner license and teen driving rules at these counties' offices. Cover the Traffic Law and Substance Abuse Education course, the one-year learner period, the 50-hour driving log, and practice limits, from the Florida Statutes and the FLHSMV teen pages. Say which local offices administer the knowledge and skills exams.
