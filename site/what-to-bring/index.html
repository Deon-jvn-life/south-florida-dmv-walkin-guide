What to bring for a Florida REAL ID driver license or ID card

From the FLHSMV U.S. citizen checklist [source]. Non-citizens: see the immigrant, non-immigrant and Canadian lists.

You must visit an office with documents for your first license or ID, if your current card has no star, or if you legally changed your name since your last card [source]. Once you have provided them, you only need them again if your information changes [source].

1. Primary identification (one original)

  • U.S. birth certificate (Puerto Rico certificates must be issued after July 1, 2010). Only county health department or CDC Bureau of Vital Statistics certificates; hospital certificates are not accepted.
  • Valid U.S. passport or passport card
  • Consular Report of Birth Abroad
  • Certificate of Naturalization (N-550 or N-570)
  • Certificate of Citizenship (N-560 or N-561)
Bring marriage certificates, court orders or divorce decrees to link names, unless your valid U.S. passport shows your current name [source].

2. Proof of Social Security number (one original)

  • Social Security card in your current name
  • W-2 (not handwritten)
  • Any 1099 (not handwritten)
The name on your SSN must match the license; allow 24 to 48 hours after changing your name with Social Security [source].

3. Two proofs of Florida residential address

Your current license does not count. Printouts are accepted. Out-of-state addresses are not accepted for a Class E license or ID [source].

  • Deed, mortgage, mortgage statement or lease
  • Florida voter registration card
  • Florida vehicle registration or title
  • Dated within the last 60 days: utility bill, auto payment booklet, selective service card, medical card or bill, home or auto insurance policy or bill, W-2, 1099 or pay stub, bank or credit card statement, government mail, or a shelter or transitional housing letter

If none are in your name, the person you live with can complete a Certification of Address (HSMV 71120) with two of their own proofs, signed in front of an examiner or a notary [source, source]. CDL applicants cannot use it [source].

Name changes

Bring the original or certified marriage certificate, divorce decree or court order; photocopies and church-issued certificates are not accepted [source].

Since May 7, 2025, TSA accepts only starred (REAL ID) licenses and IDs at airports; a U.S. passport can be used instead [source].
