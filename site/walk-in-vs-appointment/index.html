Walk-in vs appointment at South Florida driver license offices

Warning: fake appointment websites. The Miami-Dade Tax Collector warns residents about unofficial appointment websites issuing invalid confirmations, and says to schedule only through mdctaxcollector.gov and verify any questionable appointment directly with the office [source, source]. Book only on the official sites: app.mdctaxcollector.gov (Miami-Dade), appointments.browardtax.org or the FLHSMV appointment system (Broward), pbctax.gov/reservations (Palm Beach). Never pay a third party for a slot. How to spot a fake.

Conflicts and gaps in official sources (21 Oct 2026)
  • FLHSMV says standard business hours are 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. and 1 p.m. to 5 p.m., closed for lunch [source], but its own Broward office rows list 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. with no lunch break [source]. Hours below are the office rows.
  • Miami-Dade Tax Collector text says it has "assumed control of eight offices", yet its locations page lists 13 offices and FLHSMV links all 13 Miami-Dade driver license offices to the Tax Collector booking system [source, source, source].
  • Broward: a Tax Collector news post says it prepares to take over driver license services in summer 2026, while its home page on 21 Oct 2026 still says new driver license services are "coming soon" [source, source]. The Tax Collector locations page lists Fort Lauderdale (115 S. Andrews Ave), Plantation and Lauderdale Lakes [source]; FLHSMV lists Plantation as motor vehicle services only and does not list 115 S. Andrews Ave [source].
  • Virtual walk-in: the Miami-Dade launch post (May 2025) names three offices; we found no current official list of all participating offices [source].
