Fake DMV appointment websites: book only on official sites

Warning: fake appointment websites. The Miami-Dade Tax Collector warns residents about unofficial appointment websites issuing invalid confirmations, and says to schedule only through mdctaxcollector.gov and verify any questionable appointment directly with the office [source, source]. Book only on the official sites: app.mdctaxcollector.gov (Miami-Dade), appointments.browardtax.org or the FLHSMV appointment system (Broward), pbctax.gov/reservations (Palm Beach). Never pay a third party for a slot. How to spot a fake.

How to protect yourself

  • Start from the agency home page (flhsmv.gov/locations, mdctaxcollector.gov, browardtax.org, pbctax.gov) rather than a search ad.
  • The official systems above do not charge a booking fee on the pages we checked. If a site asks you to pay just to reserve a slot, leave.
  • If a confirmation looks odd, verify it directly with the office, as the Miami-Dade Tax Collector advises [source].
  • Use the exact name on your ID: Broward will not honor an appointment whose name does not match [source].
