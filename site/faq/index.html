South Florida DMV FAQ

Can I walk in at a Miami-Dade DMV?

Yes at Tax Collector offices during operating hours, but online appointments have priority. Some offices use a virtual walk-in queue by QR code [source, source].

Can I walk in at a Palm Beach DMV?

No. The Palm Beach Tax Collector requires a reservation for in-person service, for county residents only [source].

Can I walk in at a Broward DMV?

Official pages do not say. FLHSMV links Broward offices to appointment systems and says many offices require appointments [source, source].

I live in Broward. Can I book in Palm Beach?

No. Palm Beach reservations are for Palm Beach County residents; Broward Tax Collector appointments are for Broward residents [source, source].

Is it legal to buy a DMV appointment from someone?

No official site sells appointments. The Miami-Dade Tax Collector warns about unofficial appointment sites issuing invalid confirmations [source].

Can I renew online?

Many renewals and replacements can be done at MyDMV Portal; you can renew up to 18 months before expiry [source].

Do Saturday hours exist?

Yes at most Miami-Dade offices, 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. at select locations [source, source]. Broward and Palm Beach driver license offices list weekdays only [source, source].

What documents do I need for REAL ID?

One primary ID, one proof of SSN and two proofs of Florida address. See what to bring [source].
