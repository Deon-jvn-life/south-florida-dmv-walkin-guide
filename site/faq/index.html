South Florida DMV FAQ

Can I walk in at a Miami-Dade driver license office?

The Tax Collector says the offices it has taken over take walk-ins during operating hours, and that online appointments have priority. It says it has taken over eight offices and does not name them. A May 2025 post names Downtown Miami, Miami Central, and Midway Crossings for a virtual walk-in queue by QR code [source, source].

Can I walk in at a Palm Beach driver license office?

No. The Palm Beach Tax Collector requires a reservation for in-person service, for county residents only [source].

Can I walk in at a Broward driver license office?

Official pages do not say. The Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles links Broward offices to appointment systems and says many offices require appointments [source, source].

I live in Broward. Can I book in Palm Beach?

No. Palm Beach reservations are for Palm Beach County residents. Non-residents are pointed to state driver license locations [source].

Are offices closed on upcoming holidays?

State offices are listed as closed on Veterans Day, Wednesday 11 Nov 2026, Thanksgiving Day, Thursday 26 Nov 2026, the Friday after Thanksgiving, 27 Nov 2026, and Christmas Day, Friday 25 Dec 2026. County tax collector offices may not follow that list [source]. The Broward County Tax Collector says its offices will be closed on 28 Oct 2026 for a Professional Development Day, and the same page also lists an early close that day at 11:30 a.m. [source].

Is it legal to buy a DMV appointment from someone?

No official site sells appointments. The Miami-Dade Tax Collector warns about unofficial appointment sites issuing invalid confirmations [source].

Can I renew online?

Many renewals and replacements can be done at MyDMV Portal; you can renew up to 18 months before expiry [source].

Do Saturday hours exist?

Yes at most Miami-Dade offices, 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. at select locations [source, source]. Broward and Palm Beach driver license offices list weekdays only [source, source].

What documents do I need for REAL ID?

One primary ID, one proof of SSN and two proofs of Florida address. See what to bring [source].
